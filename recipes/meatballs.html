Meatballs

Ingredients

  • Ground beef: Lean ground beef is the main ingredient for juicy and flavorful meatballs.
  • Breadcrumbs: Help bind the mixture and keep the meatballs tender.
  • Parmesan cheese: Adds a rich and savory flavor.
  • Egg: Holds the ingredients together.
  • Garlic: Fresh minced garlic enhances the taste.
  • Parsley: Fresh chopped parsley adds color and freshness.
  • Milk: Keeps the meatballs moist.
  • Salt and pepper: Season the meatballs to taste.
  • Olive oil: Used for browning the meatballs before serving.

Quantities

  • 1 pound lean ground beef
  • 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil

Steps

  1. Gather all your ingredients.
  2. In a large bowl, combine the ground beef, breadcrumbs, Parmesan cheese, egg, garlic, parsley, milk, salt, and pepper.
  3. Mix the ingredients gently until well combined.
  4. Shape the mixture into evenly sized meatballs.
  5. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
  6. Add the meatballs and cook until browned on all sides.
  7. Reduce the heat and continue cooking for about 10 to 15 minutes, or until the meatballs are fully cooked.
  8. Remove the meatballs from the skillet and let them rest for a few minutes.
  9. Serve hot with tomato sauce, pasta, or your favorite side dish.
